## Prompt para o Suno

**Título:** SOBE O GRAVE

**Style of Music:**

High-energy Brazilian trap-piseiro fusion, aggressive confident male rap
performance in Brazilian Portuguese, 130 BPM, deep heavy sub-bass
"paredão" style bass drops, punchy electronic kick and bumbo, bright
piseiro synth stabs, rapid sharp flow, call-and-response hype chants
with a large crowd shouting responses, brief silence before a massive
final beat drop, energetic festival/baile anthem, motivational and
boastful lyrics, polished modern low end with piseiro bass wobble,
extremely hard-hitting production, clear vocal diction, no soft
ballad elements.

**Excluir:** soft trap balada, romantic R&B, acoustic guitar, sad
melodic chorus, reggae, deep house, female lead vocal, lo-fi drums.
