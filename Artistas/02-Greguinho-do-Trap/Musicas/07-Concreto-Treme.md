## Prompt para o Suno

**Título:** CONCRETO TREME

**Style of Music:**

Explosive Brazilian hardcore hip-hop with late-1990s East Coast energy,
original aggressive male rap performance in Brazilian Portuguese, 96 BPM,
massive distorted kick drums, crushing snare on beats two and four, dark
sub bass, ominous church bells, cinematic brass stabs, dramatic string hits,
gritty urban atmosphere, raw commanding verses, sharp rhythmic flow,
guttural hype shouts, intense call-and-response chorus with a large male
crowd yelling “TREME”, brief silence before a devastating final beat drop,
dark choir in the bridge, powerful workout and entrance anthem, rebellious
but motivational lyrics, polished modern low end, extremely hard-hitting
production, clear vocal diction, no melodic pop chorus.

**Excluir:** soft trap, romantic R&B, piseiro, sertanejo, acoustic guitar,
happy pop, female lead vocal, lo-fi drums, relaxed delivery.
