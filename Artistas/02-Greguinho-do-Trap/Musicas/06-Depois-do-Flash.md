**Style of Music:**

Contemporary Brazilian urban R&B blended with melodic trap soul and subtle
Afro-R&B influence, 85 BPM, 4/4, emotional high male lead vocal in Brazilian
Portuguese, intimate melodic rap flow, deep smooth 808 sub bass, sparse
half-time kick, crisp snare and clap on beats two and four, syncopated
percussion anticipating the downbeat, dark cinematic synth pads, emotional
piano accents, vulnerable close-mic verses, memorable soulful chorus with
layered high backing vocals, brief spoken bridge with drums stopping,
gradual 808 build and powerful final chorus, introspective story about fame,
loneliness and the person behind the artist, polished modern production,
clear diction, F major with emotional D minor undertones.
