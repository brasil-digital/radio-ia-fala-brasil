# 05 — O CELULAR NÃO SABE
**Artista:** Mell-Rillia Mel-Donça
**Estilo:** Sertanejo sofrência moderno
**Tema:** O celular continua mostrando lembranças depois do fim do relacionamento

*Mas não consegue apagar do coração.*

## Prompt para o Suno

**Título:** O CELULAR NÃO SABE

**Style of Music:**

Modern Brazilian sertanejo sofrência, powerful and deeply emotional female
lead vocal in Brazilian Portuguese, 84 BPM, intimate acoustic guitar opening,
warm piano, crying accordion, subtle bass and restrained percussion, verses
sung close to the microphone with vulnerable emotion, rising pre-chorus,
huge heartbreaking singalong chorus, memorable melodic hook, dramatic pauses,
spoken bridge with all instruments stopping, explosive final chorus with full
band and female backing vocals, authentic live bar atmosphere, polished
radio-ready production, clear diction, raw heartbreak and emotional delivery.
