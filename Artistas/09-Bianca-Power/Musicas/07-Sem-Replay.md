## Prompt para o Suno

**Título:** SEM REPLAY

**Style of Music:**

Brazilian retro dance-pop with modern R&B polish, sung in Brazilian
Portuguese by a confident young female alto, 118 BPM, glamorous nocturnal
neon atmosphere, pulsating analog synth bass, bright arpeggiated synthesizers,
punchy vintage drum machine, gated snare, subtle funk guitar, intimate and
slightly breathy verses building into a huge melodic chorus, layered female
backing vocals answering “replay”, catchy post-chorus chant, theatrical spoken
bridge with the beat dropping out, handclaps and rising synth build, explosive
final chorus with a tasteful key change, polished radio-ready production,
sensual but empowering, dramatic, danceable, memorable pop hook, clear diction

**Excluir:** sertanejo, acoustic ballad, trap rap, heavy rock guitars,
aggressive baile funk, male lead vocal, childish vocal, comedy, lo-fi production.
